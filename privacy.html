Privacy Policy

Effective 29 October 2026 · Applies to our website and to NameThatFile, Image Resizer and Image Optimizer for Mac and Windows

In short: our apps do not collect, store or send any personal data. Your files are processed only on your own computer.

Your files

The apps read the files you choose so they can rename, resize or optimize them. This happens entirely on your Mac or PC. Your files, their contents and any text read from them are never uploaded, shared or sent to us or to anyone else. The apps work without an internet connection.

Information kept on your computer

  • Settings, such as whether the completion sound is on.
  • NameThatFile’s Batch History, so Undo keeps working after you close the app. It contains file names, folder locations and file fingerprints, never file contents. You can clear it by deleting the app’s data.
  • Folder permissions (Mac): when you allow a Mac app to work in a folder, macOS gives it a permission token so it does not ask again. You can remove it by deleting the app.

This information stays on your computer and is never sent to us.

No accounts, tracking or advertising

The apps have no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party tracking.

Purchases

Free trials and purchases are handled entirely by Apple (Mac App Store) or Microsoft (Microsoft Store). The apps only ask the store whether a trial or purchase is active; the store keeps that record with your Apple Account or Microsoft account. We do not receive your name, email address or payment details. Apple’s and Microsoft’s own privacy policies apply to those transactions.

This website

Website analytics are separate from the apps. The apps do not contain analytics or upload your files.

Optional Google Analytics: if you choose Allow analytics, this website loads Google Analytics to measure page visits and actions such as clicking a store button, opening an app’s details, saving the page link or contacting us. This helps us understand which apps interest visitors. Google receives page and referrer addresses without their query strings or fragments, event categories, and technical browser/device and connection information. We do not send your files, document text, names, email addresses or message contents as analytics event data.

Analytics uses _ga and related _ga_ cookies to distinguish browsers and visits. We configure these cookies to expire after 180 days without extending that duration on every visit. Advertising personalization and Google signals are disabled. We do not use Google Ads, session recordings or advertising pixels.

Your choice: analytics is off until you consent. If you choose No thanks, the Google tag does not load and no analytics measurements are sent. The site stores your analytics preference in your browser for up to 180 days so it can remember your choice. You can change it using Privacy settings in the footer. Withdrawing consent stops further collection and removes the analytics cookies set by this site; it does not automatically remove measurements already sent to Google.

Google processes analytics information on our behalf and may process it outside the EEA under its applicable transfer safeguards. See Google’s privacy policy and how Google Analytics safeguards data. Analytics reports include only visitors who allow measurement and may be affected by browser blocking.

Hosting and contact: the site is hosted by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.), which may keep standard technical logs, such as IP addresses, for security and operation; see GitHub’s privacy statement. If you email us, we use your message and email address to reply and keep them only as long as needed to help you.

Children

The apps collect no information from anyone, including children.

Your rights

The apps do not send us personal data. For personal data processed through this website or because you emailed us, you can ask to access, correct or delete it, and exercise any other applicable data protection rights by contacting us below. We do not receive a named visitor list from Analytics and may be unable to identify your browser’s measurements from your name or email alone. You can withdraw analytics consent at any time. You may complain to your local data protection authority, including Ireland’s Data Protection Commission.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
