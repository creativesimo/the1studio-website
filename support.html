TidyTuck for iPhone

TidyTuck keeps everything you buy in one place: each item with its receipts, photos, return date and warranty. It needs an iPhone with iOS 18 or later.

How do I add things?

Tap + in the middle of the bottom bar. Take a photo of a receipt, tag, barcode or order screenshot, choose photos or files (or a whole folder), or type an item in. To gather receipts already on your iPhone, tap Search my iPhone. TidyTuck fills in what it can read; check the details before saving.

Is anything uploaded?

No. Receipts and photos are read on your iPhone, and there is no account or cloud service. See the privacy policy.

How do I move to a new iPhone?

Setting up your new iPhone from an iCloud backup, a computer backup or Quick Start brings TidyTuck’s data across with your other apps. You can also go to Settings → Data & privacy → Make a backup file in TidyTuck, save it to iCloud Drive or a computer, and use Restore from a backup on the new iPhone.

I deleted TidyTuck. Can I get my items back?

Deleting the app removes its data from your iPhone. You can bring it back by restoring a TidyTuck backup file, or by restoring your iPhone from a backup made before the app was deleted. We can’t recover it for you, because nothing is stored on our side, so please make a backup from time to time.

Reminders don’t appear

Check that notifications are allowed for TidyTuck in iOS Settings → Notifications, and that the date has a reminder set. Focus modes can also hide alerts.

Search my iPhone doesn’t find my receipts

TidyTuck can only read photos you allow. In iOS Settings → TidyTuck → Photos, choose Full Access or add the photos you want. Photos stored only in iCloud need to download first.

Purchases

TidyTuck is a one-time purchase on the App Store, with no subscription. To install it on another iPhone, sign in with the same Apple Account and download it again from the App Store. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

NameThatFile, Image Resizer and Image Optimizer

Each app also has built-in help: on Mac choose Help in the menu bar; on Windows press F1 or click the ? button.

How does the free trial work?

Every app works fully for 7 days, and nothing is charged when the trial ends. After that, a one-time purchase of $4.99 unlocks it for good, including future updates. There is no subscription. The store shows the exact price in your currency, including local taxes, before you buy.

I bought an app. How do I restore my purchase?

Purchases belong to your Apple or Microsoft account. Sign in with the same account in the Mac App Store or Microsoft Store and download the app again; it unlocks automatically. On a Mac, you can also choose Restore Purchase in the app.

Are my files uploaded anywhere?

No. Everything happens on your computer, and the apps work without an internet connection. See our privacy policy.

Will the apps change my original files?

  • NameThatFile changes only the file name. Files stay in their folder with their extension and contents unchanged, and Undo This Batch or Batch History restores the original names.
  • Image Resizer and Image Optimizer never change originals. They save new copies in a Resized or Optimized folder beside them.

Why does a Mac app ask for permission to use a folder?

Apps from the Mac App Store can only change files you choose. NameThatFile renames files inside their folder, and Image Resizer and Image Optimizer save copies next to your images, so the first time each folder is used the app asks you to click Allow. It remembers your answer for that folder.

Why did NameThatFile skip a file?

Click Details… in the app to see why. Common reasons are unsupported file types, hidden files, files open in another app, or cloud files that are only online. For OneDrive files on Windows, right-click the file and choose Always keep on this device first.

What do I need?

  • iPhone (TidyTuck): iOS 18 or later.
  • Mac: macOS 13 Ventura or later, on Apple silicon or Intel.
  • Windows: Windows 10 (version 2004 or later) or Windows 11, on Intel, AMD or Arm.
